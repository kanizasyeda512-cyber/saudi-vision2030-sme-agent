# Problem Selection

## Project
Saudi Vision 2030 SME Growth AI Agent

## Selected Problem
Limited access to suitable financing and business guidance for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Saudi Arabia.

## Problem Description
Small businesses may face challenges in finding suitable funding options, understanding business requirements, and planning sustainable growth.

## Proposed AI Solution
An AI-powered SME Support Agent that helps small businesses by:

1. Understanding business needs.
2. Suggesting suitable financing pathways.
3. Providing business growth guidance.
4. Recommending relevant support resources.
5. Mapping recommendations to UN Sustainable Development Goals.

## Saudi Vision 2030 Alignment
The project supports economic diversification, entrepreneurship, private-sector growth, and SME development.

## Relevant SDGs
- SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth
- SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
- SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals

## Expected Impact
Help SMEs make informed business decisions and identify opportunities for sustainable growth.
