## Chosen Problem
**Youth & Female Workforce Skill-Matching and Nitaqat Compliance under the Human Capability Development Program (HCDP).**

## Target Users
- Saudi job seekers and national graduates seeking aligned placement.
- Corporate HR leaders managing Saudization quotas and talent acquisition.
- Policy analysts evaluating national employment KPIs.

## Vision 2030 Pillar & VRP
- **Pillar:** Thriving Economy & Vibrant Society
- **VRP:** Human Capability Development Program (HCDP) & National Transformation Program (NTP)
- **KPI:** Saudi Unemployment Rate down to 7% and Female Labor Force Participation surpassing 35%.

## Problem Statement
Rapid economic diversification in Saudi Arabia requires balancing high-value job creation with strict Saudization (Nitaqat) mandates. Enterprises frequently experience friction matching qualified national talent—particularly expanding cohorts of female STEM graduates—to operational roles, risking lower Nitaqat tier rankings. This autonomous AI agent bridges enterprise vacancies with qualified candidates while optimizing compliance and providing bilingual advisory support.
