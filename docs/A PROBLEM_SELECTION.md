# Project
Saudi Vision 2030 SME Growth AI Agent

## Selected Problem
Limited access to suitable financing and business guidance for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Saudi Arabia.

## Problem Description
Small businesses may face challenges in finding suitable funding options, understanding business requirements, and planning sustainable growth.

## Proposed AI Solution
An AI-powered SME Support Agent that helps small businesses by:
- Analyzing funding and licensing requirements.
- Providing structured recommendations aligned with Saudi Vision 2030.
- Assisting entrepreneurs in both Arabic and English.
