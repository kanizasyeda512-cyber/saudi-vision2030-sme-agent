## Risks & Ethics (Saudi PDPL Compliance)
- **Data Privacy:** Full compliance with the **Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)**. All candidate records and enterprise personnel metrics are anonymized and processed locally or securely encrypted.
- **Algorithmic Fairness & Bias Mitigation:** Regular auditing of skill recommendation weights to ensure gender-neutral and nationality-neutral meritocratic matching.
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** The agent acts as an advisory assistant. Final hiring decisions and official Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) submissions remain strictly under human supervision.
