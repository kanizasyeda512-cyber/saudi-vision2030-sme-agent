# Data Provenance

All sample datasets in `data/sample/` are structured to mirror authentic workforce metrics from the General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) and Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) Nitaqat guidelines under Saudi Vision 2030.

- `candidates.json`: Synthetic profiles reflecting Saudi youth and female professionals across Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam.
- `enterprises.json`: Enterprise tier structures for retail and IT sectors under Nitaqat employment bands.
